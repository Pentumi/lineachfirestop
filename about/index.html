About

Lineach Construction Limited

Trading as Líneach Fire Stop. Install-only fire stopping for main contractors and M&E.

What the company is

Líneach Fire Stop is the fire stopping package of Lineach Construction Limited. We supply labour and certified materials and we install to drawings and specifications issued by others.

We are based at Ardnamullan, Clonard, Co. Meath, A83 FC42 — Enfield / Summerhill. The working area is Dublin and the surrounding counties: Meath, Kildare, Wicklow and Louth.

How we work

The market is full of firms who survey, design, install and certify as one wrap. That is not this company. Design duty and surveys sit elsewhere. If a client needs them, a design partner can take that on a separate contract. Our subcontract stays install-only, with no design responsibility written in.

On site: SOLAS Safe Pass, RAMS, insurance certificates and tax clearance as main contractors require. We install manufacturer-tested systems named on the spec. We hand over photos, a seal register and a construction ancillary certificate for install-to-details only.

Who we talk to

Site managers, M&E contract managers, and QS / buying on the main contractor side. If you already have a fire strategy and details, we can price. If you do not, we are the wrong first call.
