What we install

Three install packages

Quoted from issued details. Missing details are referred to the design party. Each package has its own page.

After M&E

Penetration sealing

  • Pipes, cables, trays and ducts through compartment walls and floors
  • Installed after first-fix, to the named tested system
  • Tags and IDs to match the seal register
Penetration sealing
Linear

Linear joint seals

  • Head-of-wall, floor-to-wall and construction joints as drawn
  • Movement joints where a tested system is named
  • Catch-up visits quoted as attendances, not assumed
Linear joint seals
Voids

Cavity barriers

  • Not a façade survey or a hunt for missing barriers

Why Líneach

Site process, not marketing claims

We do not publish project counts or star ratings. What we can show a main contractor is the paperwork they already ask for.

SOLAS Safe Pass

Operatives hold Safe Pass. RAMS are issued before works commence, against the site you name.

BCAR install ancillary

Construction ancillary for installation to issued details only. Photo log, seal register and product evidence for the Assigned Certifier. Not a design ancillary.

Tax and insurance

Insurance certificates and tax clearance are supplied as part of the appointment process, when the main contractor requires them.

Irish TGD B / Part B

Compartmentation at service openings as TGD B requires. Irish BCAR forms. We do not work to UK Approved Document B.

Scope of works

What is in

You hold the fire strategy, specification and drawings. We install to them. The quotation is labour and certified materials against the issued design. Print the IN / OUT page with a quotation if useful.

IN

Labour

Site labour to install the seals shown on the issued drawings. Operatives hold SOLAS Safe Pass. RAMS are issued before works commence.

IN

Certified materials

Manufacturer-tested systems named on your specification. Product evidence — data sheets, Declarations of Performance, and test / ETA documents — as specified.

IN

To issued details

Penetrations, linear joints and cavity barriers as drawn. Missing details are referred to the design party. We do not invent or substitute details on site.

Scope exclusions

What is out

Exclusions are stated so the subcontract matches the works.

OUT

No design

We do not select systems or approve the fire strategy. Variations that require a new detail are referred to the designer.

OUT

No surveys

We do not survey buildings to locate openings. A find-all-penetrations duty is not included in the quotation.

OUT

No design duty

The contract states that we carry no design responsibility. We do not hold professional indemnity for design. We do not sign design ancillaries.

OUT

Design partner

Design or survey can sit with a partner on a separate contract. The quotation remains for installation only.
