Scope of works

What is in

You hold the fire strategy, specification and drawings. We install to them. The quotation is labour and certified materials against the issued design.

IN

Labour

Site labour to install the seals shown on the issued drawings. Operatives hold SOLAS Safe Pass. RAMS are issued before works commence.

IN

Certified materials

Manufacturer-tested systems named on your specification. Product evidence — data sheets, Declarations of Performance, and test / ETA documents — as specified.

IN

To issued details

Penetrations, linear joints and cavity barriers as drawn. Missing details are referred to the design party. We do not invent or substitute details on site.

Scope exclusions

What is out

Exclusions are stated so the subcontract matches the works. Print the IN / OUT page with a quotation if useful.

OUT

No design

We do not select systems or approve the fire strategy. Variations that require a new detail are referred to the designer.

OUT

No surveys

We do not survey buildings to locate openings. A find-all-penetrations duty is not included in the quotation.

OUT

No design duty

The contract states that we carry no design responsibility. We do not hold professional indemnity for design. We do not sign design ancillaries.

OUT

Design partner

Design or survey can sit with a partner on a separate contract. The quotation remains for installation only.

How a quotation is prepared

01

Submit the documents

Drawings, fire specification and schedules. We price from what is issued.

02

Labour and materials

Certified systems named on the specification. Not a design fee.

03

Provisional sums

Unspecified openings are treated as provisional. We do not search the building for additional openings.

04

Handover pack

Photographs, seal register, and an install-to-details ancillary for the Assigned Certifier.

Documents

What you send

A quotation is prepared from the pack you issue. If a document is not available, say so — we will state the assumption or a provisional sum.

  • Issued drawings — compartmentation, services, details
  • Fire specification or fire strategy extracts
  • Penetration or joint schedules where they exist
  • Named tested systems, if already specified
  • Programme window and site access notes
  • County / site address

BCAR

Handover artefacts

Install-only does not mean an absence of paperwork. It means we do not issue a design certificate. The Assigned Certifier still requires evidence that the installation matches the issued details.

Photo log

Before, during and after. Referenced to seal IDs so the pack can be read against the register.

Seal register

ID, location, system, date, installer. The record used by the O&M file and the Assigned Certifier.

Product file

Data sheets, Declarations of Performance, and fire test / ETA evidence for the systems installed.
