Scope of works

Install-only fire stopping

You hold the fire strategy, specification and drawings. We install to them. Our scope is limited to installation against the issued design.

Labour

Site labour to install the seals shown on the issued drawings. All operatives hold SOLAS Safe Pass. RAMS are issued before works commence.

Certified materials

Manufacturer-tested systems named on your specification. Product evidence — data sheets, Declarations of Performance, and test / ETA documents — as specified.

To issued details

Penetrations, linear joints and cavity barriers as drawn. Missing details are referred to the design party. We do not invent or substitute details on site.

Scope exclusions

What we do not do

Scope exclusions are stated clearly so the subcontract matches the works.

No design

We do not select systems or approve the fire strategy. Variations that require a new detail are referred to the designer.

No surveys

We do not survey buildings to locate openings. A find-all-penetrations duty is not included in our quotation.

No design duty

The contract states that we carry no design responsibility. We do not hold professional indemnity for design. We do not sign design ancillaries.

Design partner

Design or survey can sit with a partner on a separate contract. Our quotation remains for installation only.

Clients

Who this is for

Main contractors who hold a firestop package and already have issued details.

M&E subcontractors who require penetration sealing after pipes, cables and ducts are installed.

New-build and fit-out packages. We are not a survey-led remediation practice.
