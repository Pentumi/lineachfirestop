The offer

Install-only firestop

You hold the fire strategy, specification and drawings. We install to them. That is the whole job.

Labour

Site labour to seal what is shown. Safe Pass on everyone who sets foot on site. RAMS issued before start.

Certified materials

Manufacturer-tested systems named on your spec. Product evidence — data sheets, DoPs, test / ETA papers — as specified.

To issued details

Penetrations, joints and cavity barriers as drawn. If a detail is missing, it goes back to the design party. We do not fill the gap.

Scope line

What we do not do

Say this up front so the subcontract matches the work.

No design

We do not select systems or sign off the fire strategy. Variations that need a new detail go back to the designer.

No surveys

We do not walk a building to find openings. Find-all-penetrations is not in our quote.

No design duty

The contract states no design responsibility. We do not carry professional indemnity for design. We do not sign design ancillaries.

Partner if needed

Design or survey can sit with a partner on a separate contract. We still only quote install.
