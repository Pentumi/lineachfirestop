Linear

Joints

Linear gap seals and movement joints where the drawings show a fire-resisting joint system. Head-of-wall, floor-to-wall, and construction joints as detailed.

As drawn

We install the joint system shown on the issued details. That includes linear gap seals at compartment lines and movement joints where a tested system is named.

We do not invent a joint detail on site. If the specification is silent, the query is referred to the design party. We then install what they issue.

Programme

Head-of-wall and perimeter joints often sit after drylining. We plan labour against the programme you issue. Catch-up visits are quoted as attendances, not assumed.
