Privacy policy

Short policy for a small Irish contractor. Last updated 19 August 2026.

Lineach Construction Limited (we), Ardnamullan, Clonard, Co. Meath, A83 FC42, is the controller for personal data sent through lineachfirestop.com.

What we collect

If you use the quote form or email us, we receive the name, company, email, phone, area and project details you type. Phone calls to +353 87 919 9059 are ordinary business calls. We do not run advertising cookies or a tracking pixel on this site.

Why we use it

To reply to a quote or a question, and to keep a record of the enquiry and any contract that follows. Legal basis: steps before a contract, and our legitimate interest in running the trade.

Who sees it

The form is processed by our host (Netlify) and then by us. We do not sell enquiry lists. We may share details with a design or survey partner only if you ask us to introduce one, or if a live job requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become a job: up to 24 months. Contracts, invoices and site records: for the period Irish tax and construction records require.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or erasure where the law allows. Email dean@lineach.com. You can complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Hosting

The website is hosted on Netlify. See their privacy notice for server logs. Pages are public. Do not put sensitive personal data in the project-details box.
