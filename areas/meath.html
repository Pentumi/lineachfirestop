County Meath

Fire stopping from a Meath base

Lineach Construction Limited is based at Ardnamullan, Clonard, A83 FC42, in the Enfield and Summerhill area — a Meath address, not a Dublin depot.

Local, install-only

Meath projects do not wait on a crew travelling from Dublin. Navan, Trim, Dunshaughlin, Dunboyne, Ashbourne, Ratoath, Kells, Enfield and the M3 / M4 industrial strips are within the home working area.

The work is the same as Dublin: penetration sealing, linear joints and cavity barriers to issued drawings. Main contractors on industrial, education, healthcare and housing projects. M&E firms who require openings closed after services.

Typical Meath projects

Logistics and light industrial along the corridors. Residential schemes where the fire strategy has already been submitted for the Fire Safety Certificate. Occasional public and education projects. We quote labour and certified materials from the specification. We do not offer a county-wide survey service.

Paperwork for BCAR

Meath Assigned Certifiers receive the same pack as a city project: photographs, seal register, product file, and a construction ancillary stating installation to issued details only. TGD B / Part B. No UK forms.

If your Meath site still requires design, that work should remain on a separate contract with a design partner. We remain on installation. See other counties or request a quote.
