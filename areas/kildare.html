Maynooth, Leixlip, Celbridge, Naas, Newbridge, Kildare town and the M4 / M7 corridor. Install to issued details.

Corridor work

Kildare sits on our doorstep. The base at Clonard is on the Meath–Kildare edge. North Kildare — Maynooth, Kilcock, Leixlip, Celbridge — is a local run. Naas, Newbridge, Sallins and the M7 parks are a straight shot.

Packages here are often warehouse and commercial compartmentation, plus residential and campus work. M&E penetration sealing after first-fix is the usual ask. We price that from schedules and drawings, not from a walk-around.

Passive fire protection install — not a design house

Kildare jobs still go through Fire Safety Certificates and BCAR. The fire strategy and the tested systems belong to the design team. We install them. If a detail is missing on a Newbridge shed or a Maynooth core, it is a design query, not a site invention.

You get the handover pack: photos, seal register, install ancillary. Irish TGD B language.
