County Louth

Firestop installation, Louth

Drogheda, Dundalk and the M1 towns. Labour and certified materials to issued details. The same install-only offer as Dublin.

M1 corridor

Louth is within the working area from the Meath base via the M3 / M1. Drogheda, Dundalk, Ardee, Dunleer and the industrial strips along the motorway are treated as standard coverage — not as exceptional travel.

Packages are typically M&E penetration sealing on commercial, industrial and residential schemes where the fire strategy and details already exist. We quote from drawings and schedules. We do not offer a county-wide survey of existing buildings.

BCAR pack

Louth Assigned Certifiers receive the same install pack: photographs, seal register, product evidence, and a construction ancillary for installation to issued details only. TGD B / Part B. Not UK Approved Document B.

If design or survey is still required, that work sits with a partner on a separate contract. Our quotation remains for installation. See all counties or request a quotation and select Louth.

Typical packages

  • Industrial and logistics compartmentation
  • M&E penetrations after first-fix
  • Residential schemes with issued details
