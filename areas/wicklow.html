County Wicklow

Fire stopping contractors, Wicklow

Bray, Greystones, Kilcoole, Wicklow town, Arklow and Blessington. The same install-only offer as Dublin.

East coast and west Wicklow

We cover Wicklow from the Meath base via the M50 / N11 for the coast and via Blessington for the west. The county is within the working area and is treated as a standard coverage county.

Wicklow packages tend to be smaller commercial, residential and some public or education work. The requirement is the same: issued drawings. Coastal fit-outs and inland schemes are both quoted where the details exist.

Penetration sealing and handover

M&E openings through compartment walls and floors are the typical scope. Joints and cavity barriers when they appear on the drawing. See services.

Handover is prepared for Irish BCAR: photo log, seal register, construction ancillary for installation to issued details only. TGD B / Part B. Not Approved Document B.

If you are the main contractor or M&E subcontractor on a Wicklow site, please send the specification. Neighbouring counties: Dublin, Kildare, Meath. Get a quote.
