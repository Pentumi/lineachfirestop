Firestop installation from a Meath base. Main contractors and M&E subcontractors on sites within a working-day radius.

Location

We are based at Ardnamullan, Clonard, Co. Meath, A83 FC42 — Enfield / Summerhill, on the Meath–Kildare boundary. West Dublin, north Kildare and the remainder of Meath are readily accessible. The M4, M7, M3 and M50 place the rest of the coverage area within range.

We do not advertise a nationwide service. If the site is in Dublin, Meath, Kildare, Wicklow or Louth, please enquire. Sites outside that area will be declined.

The same offer in every county: penetration sealing, joints and cavity barriers to issued details. No design. No survey.
