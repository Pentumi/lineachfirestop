Dublin

Firestop install Dublin

Penetration sealing and passive fire protection install for Dublin sites. Labour and certified materials to the drawings you already have.

Where the work sits

Most firestop packages we are asked about are in Dublin. Office and commercial fit-out. New-build cores and risers after M&E. Student and residential schemes where the fire strategy is already approved and the details exist.

The buyer is usually the main contractor with a specialist package, or the M&E subcontractor who opened the walls and now needs them sealed. That is fire stopping contractors Ireland work as it is actually bought — not a walk-round survey of an occupied block.

How we get there

We travel from Clonard / Enfield. West and north Dublin — Blanchardstown, Citywest, the airport corridor, Swords, Fingal — is a direct run. South and city-centre sites come off the M50. We plan attendances around your programme, including evening or weekend sealing when the floor is clear, if the quote says so.

What we need on a Dublin job

Compartmentation drawings. The fire specification or the relevant TGD B details. Penetration schedules if the M&E has them. We quote from that pack. We do not take a Dublin site on the basis of come-and-find-the-holes.

Existing apartment remediation is usually survey-led. That is not our day-to-day offer. If a design or survey partner already holds that contract, we can quote the install that follows.

What you get

The same install scope as every other county: tested systems as specified, photo log, seal register, construction ancillary for install-to-details only. Irish BCAR paperwork. Not UK Approved Document B language.

Meath, Kildare and Wicklow use the same model. Get a Dublin quote.

  • Riser and corridor catch-up
  • Fit-out compartment lines
